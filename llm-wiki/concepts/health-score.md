# Health Score

A health score summarizes Phase 1 repository hygiene for skills and registries. It helps maintainers identify validation problems and review priorities.

Phase 1 scoring is basic. It reflects structural checks, registry consistency, manifest quality signals, and generated findings.

Findings have different severity levels:

- errors identify blockers that should be fixed before completion;
- warnings identify visible problems or risks that may not block all Phase 1 work;
- info findings provide context or low-severity notes.

The health score does not measure end-to-end skill quality, real-world task success, user satisfaction, or production reliability. Future phases may expand health with evaluation results and observability signals, but those integrations are not implemented in Phase 1.
