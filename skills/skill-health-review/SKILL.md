# Skill Health Review Skill

## How to interpret health score
Scores near 100 indicate strong Phase 1 hygiene. Lower scores identify missing governance or documentation fields.

## How to prioritize errors
Fix validation errors before warnings because errors block registry trust and CI.

## How to interpret warnings
Warnings identify acceptable Phase 1 gaps such as unconfigured evaluation suites or draft status.

## How to prepare review comments
Reference the exact finding code, file path, expected change, and owner responsible for follow-up.

## How to derive improvement suggestions
Map score deductions to concrete actions such as adding SKILL.md, assigning ownership, or moving from draft to reviewed.
